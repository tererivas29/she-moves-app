Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This policy describes how the BTR (Build Track Repeat) Android app handles your data.

What data we collect

This app stores the following data, and it stays on your device unless you choose otherwise:

  • Workout and exercise data you enter (sets, reps, weights, notes)
  • Exercise images you add to the app

Where data is stored

All data is stored in two places, both under your control:

  • On your device — in a local SQLite database.
  • In your own Google Drive — if you enable backup, data is synced to a folder in your personal Google Drive account. We never have access to this data.

Google Drive access

This app requests access to Google Drive solely to back up and restore your own workout data. We only read and write files created by this app. We do not access any other files in your Drive.

Crash and diagnostic reports

To find and fix crashes, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes, it sends:

  • the stack trace of the crash
  • the state of the app at the moment it crashed
  • device information such as model and operating system version
  • a randomly generated installation identifier, used only to count how many users a crash affects

This goes to Google, which processes it on our behalf so that we can fix the problem. It is not linked to your name, your email address, or your workout data. It is sent automatically, and there is currently no setting in the app to turn it off. Google retains these reports according to Firebase's data retention policy.

Video links you add

You can attach a video link to an exercise. When you play it, the app opens that link in a browser window inside the app. The site you linked to — YouTube, or any other site you pasted — receives that request and may set its own cookies, exactly as it would in any browser. We do not send your workout data to it.

Sharing a workout

When you use the share button, you choose which app receives the summary, and the data goes wherever you send it. We are not involved in that transfer and we do not receive a copy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • The app contains no advertising SDK, and no analytics or tracking SDK. We do not measure how you use the app.
  • We run no servers of our own, so there is no account with us, and your workout data is never sent to us.
  • The only data that leaves your device to a service we chose is the crash reporting described above.

Data deletion

Your workout data: you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app and removing the backup folder from your Google Drive. Both locations are under your control.

Crash reports: these contain nothing that identifies you, so they cannot be looked up or deleted individually. They are retained by Google according to Firebase's data retention policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, this policy is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
