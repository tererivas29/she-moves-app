Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

What data we collect

This app collects and stores the following data:

  • Workout and exercise data you enter (sets, reps, weights, notes)
  • Exercise images you add to the app

Where data is stored

All data is stored in two places, both under your control:

  • On your device — in a local SQLite database.
  • In your own Google Drive — if you enable backup, data is synced to a folder in your personal Google Drive account. We never have access to this data.

Google Drive access

This app requests access to Google Drive solely to back up and restore your own workout data. We only read and write files created by this app. We do not access any other files in your Drive.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third parties. Your data never passes through our servers.

Data deletion

You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app and removing the backup folder from your Google Drive.
